# Highlights

- First synthesis and test of an LLM-hypothesized Fe-Mn-Al-Ni-Si-C shape memory alloy
- AI alloy shows no super-elastic or shape-memory recovery under any anneal tested
- Omori benchmark recovers ≈0.5% strain on heating, confirming martensite reversion
- Rietveld refinement: AI alloy is 62% FCC + 34% BCC + 4% DO3 Fe3Al, no B2 precipitates
- Elevated carbon raises FCC stability, leaving too little BCC for transformation
