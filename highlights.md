# Highlights

- An LLM-hypothesized Fe-Mn-Al-Ni-Si-C alloy was cast, drawn to wire, and tested
- The LLM-alloy shows no super-elastic or shape-memory recovery in any tested state
- Benchmark alloy recovers ≈0.5% strain on heating, validating the test protocol
- Rietveld: 62% γ, 34% α, 4% D0₃ and no B2 — too little parent phase to transform
- CALPHAD in three databases: the measured carbon closes the α field below solidus
