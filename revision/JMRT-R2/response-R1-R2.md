# Responses to Reviewers 1 and 2 — JMRT-D-26-06169R1, Round 2

JMRT takes one response per reviewer. These two are short because both reviewers closed
their reviews; they are kept separate from the Reviewer-3 response so that neither is sent
a reply they cannot see.

---

## Reviewer 1

> No comment.

We thank Reviewer 1 for the time given to both rounds of this manuscript. The changes made
in this revision respond to Reviewer 3 and to an error we found in our own calculations
(Sec. 3.4: the liquid phase had been omitted from two of the three thermodynamic databases;
the corrected calculations are described in the response to Reviewer 3, point 6, and the

## Reviewer 2

> I am pleased to state that the authors have comprehensively and satisfactorily addressed
> all the points raised in my initial review. … I recommend acceptance of this paper in its
> current form.

We thank Reviewer 2 for the careful reading of both versions and for the recommendation.
Since that review the manuscript has been revised once more in response to Reviewer 3 and to
correct an error of our own in the CALPHAD calculations of Sec. 3.4 (the liquid phase had
been omitted from two of the three databases). The correction strengthens rather than
weakens the point Reviewer 2 asked us to support by calculation in Round 1: with the liquid
phase included, the LLM-alloy begins to melt before it is ever single-phase α in any of the
three databases, while the carbon-free control remains single-phase α at 1200 °C. Every
changed sentence is marked in the tracked-changes file, and the full account is given in the
response to Reviewer 3, point 6.
