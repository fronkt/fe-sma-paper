Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript, *Absence of Super-elasticity in an LLM-Hypothesized Fe-Mn-Al-Ni-Si-C
Alloy: Tensile, Synchrotron and CALPHAD Assessment*, for consideration as a peer-reviewed paper
in the *Journal of Materials Engineering and Performance*.

Large language model (LLM) research agents can now propose alloy compositions in minutes, but
very few of those proposals have been taken to a furnace. We melted, drew and heat-treated one
Fe-Mn-Al-Ni-Si-C shape memory alloy composition drawn from an LLM agent's design report. We then
evaluated it against an Omori-type Fe-Mn-Al-Ni benchmark processed on the same route, using
cyclic tensile tests across a 600–1200 °C anneal series, a loading–unloading–heating test,
synchrotron diffraction with Rietveld refinement, optical and SEM/EDS metallography, and
equilibrium calculations in three independent thermodynamic databases.

The alloy is strong (0.2 % proof stress 1908–519 MPa across the anneal series) but shows no
super-elastic or shape-memory recovery in any condition tested, while the benchmark does. The
manuscript keeps three kinds of evidence separate. **Measured:** the absence of recovery, and a
γ-majority duplex microstructure after the quench. **Calculated:** in all three databases the
composition has no single-phase α field in the solid state, and deleting its carbon opens one.
**Open:** whether the γ excess forms at temperature or on cooling. The composition made lies
outside the agent's proposed window in Mn and Al, and the paper says so where it is introduced.
The practical conclusion for materials engineers is a screening step that costs nothing in the
laboratory: check an LLM-proposed composition for competing-phase stability and interstitial
content before it is melted.

The work fits the journal's scope on testing and characterization and on the relationship
between processing and performance. The design report the composition was taken from is
supplied as Online Resource 1. A specimen-by-specimen register of every record behind the
figures and tables, together with the calculation settings, is supplied as Online Resource 2.

An earlier version of this work, *Mechanical Responses of an AI-Hypothesized Super-elastic
Fe-Mn-Al-Ni-Si-C Alloy*, was submitted to the journal on 13 July 2026 as JMEP-26-07-50730; we did
not return its copyright transfer form. The present manuscript is a substantially revised version
that supersedes it, and we ask that JMEP-26-07-50730 be withdrawn. Apart from that earlier
version, this manuscript has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere.
Preliminary results were presented as a poster at SMST 2026. All authors have approved the
submission.
