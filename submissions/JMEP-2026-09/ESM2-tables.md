## Table S1. Specimen and record register

Heats: **RD0697-6** = LLM-alloy; **RD0697-7** = benchmark alloy. Diameters are the values entered in the test record for that specimen (nominal wire size in brackets where it differs). "Identity" states what on the primary record ties the specimen to its alloy: a heat number printed per specimen, a composition label printed per specimen, or the heat number under which the whole report is filed. Tensile tests: room temperature, displacement control, strain = crosshead extension / gauge length without extensometry, stress from the recorded diameter (Sec. 2.4). "Cyclic tension" = loading in successive increments of nominally 1% strain with an unloading after each, up to ≈8–12% strain or earlier fracture, then loading to fracture, at a crosshead speed of 0.25 in min⁻¹ where the record states it (M2, M3, M6–M13). Unknown cells are marked *not recorded*.

## Table S2. Calculation register

All calculations: pycalphad 0.11.2, 1 atm, liquid phase included, databases as distributed in the pycalphad open multicomponent-database collection. Elements a database does not carry were omitted and the remainder renormalized (Sec. 2.6). "pdens" is pycalphad's starting-grid density in points per degree of freedom. Starting grids were pdens 500 (mpea-02b, PrecHiMn-04) or 2000 (mc_fe). In the high-temperature bands of the step diagrams (mpea-02b: benchmark from 1300 °C, LLM-alloy from 1250 °C, C-free LLM-alloy from 1350 °C; PrecHiMn-04: LLM-alloy from 1150 °C, C-free LLM-alloy from 1280 °C; all to 1400 °C) the lowest-Gibbs-energy state across density ladders spanning pdens 300–4000 was retained. The Ni scans (K4, K5) and A2-window runs (K6, K7) were not arbitrated.
