Dear Editor,

Please find enclosed our revised manuscript for JMRT-D-26-06169, resubmitted in
response to the decision of major revision. We thank you and the three reviewers
for a detailed and constructive set of 45 comments; the manuscript is
substantially stronger for them.

The revision responds to every comment. The principal changes are:

1. **New thermodynamic analysis.** A CALPHAD study (new Secs. 2.6 and 3.4, new
   Fig. 9) computes equilibrium phase fractions for both alloys, an in-silico
   carbon-free control, and a nickel scan across three independent databases. It
   shows that at the measured carbon level the LLM-alloy has no single-phase α
   field at any temperature its processing can reach — the mechanism behind the
   absent super-elasticity, requested independently by Reviewers 1 and 3.

2. **The abnormal-grain-growth result is now reported in full** (new Sec. 3.5,
   new Figs. 8 and 10) rather than in a single sentence, with post-AGG
   diffraction, micrographs and replicate mechanical data.

3. **New characterization.** SEM/EDS phase identification, quantitative Rietveld
   phase fractions with texture analysis (new Fig. 7), measured interstitial
   carbon for both heats, and a rebuilt Fig. 2 showing all eight heat-treated
   conditions from the raw load-frame exports.

4. **Tempered claims and naming.** The title is revised to *Experimental
   Validation of an LLM-Hypothesized Fe-Mn-Al-Ni-Si-C Alloy: Phase Stability
   Governs the Absence of Super-elasticity*; the test alloy is now the
   "LLM-alloy" throughout (resolving an AI/Al typeface ambiguity raised by
   Reviewer 1); and the benchmark is framed explicitly as a control.

5. **Provenance.** The complete 41-page LLM design report is supplied as
   Supplementary Material, and the generative-AI declaration is updated.

Because the reviews are answered per reviewer, we upload three response
documents, each quoting that reviewer's comments verbatim and answering them in
order. The marked-up manuscript shows every change against the submitted
version.

This work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors
have approved the revised version. We hope the manuscript is now suitable for
publication in the *Journal of Materials Research and Technology*.
