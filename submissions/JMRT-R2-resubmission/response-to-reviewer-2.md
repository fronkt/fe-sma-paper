We thank Reviewer 2 for the careful reading of both versions and for the recommendation.
Since that review the manuscript has been revised once more in response to Reviewer 3 and to
correct two errors of our own: the liquid phase had been omitted from two of the three
databases in the CALPHAD calculations of Sec. 3.4, and one 40-minute-anneal elongation in
Sec. 3.5 had been taken from a benchmark test record. The first correction strengthens rather
than weakens the point Reviewer 2 asked us to support by calculation in Round 1: with the
liquid phase included, the LLM-alloy begins to melt before it is ever single-phase α in any of
the three databases, while the carbon-free control remains single-phase α at 1200 °C. The
second is replaced by the LLM-alloy's own record, which supports the same conclusion, and a
new Fig. 10 presents every tensile record in those conditions directly. Every changed sentence
is marked in the tracked-changes file, and the full account is given in the response to
Reviewer 3, points 6 and 7.
